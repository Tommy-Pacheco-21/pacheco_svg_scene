The Neverending Night of Moonlight City

A 500×300 cityscape scene built with rectangles, a circle, polygons, and text. Each mountain is a polygon; the sky, ground, buildings, and windows are rectangles; the sun is a circle. All mountains share a stroke via the <g> group.

Reflection: Unlike most other previous projects, I went into this assignment completely blind, not knowing what the S or the V or the G means in SVG. To be honest, I actually had a lot of fun working on this project, getting to practice my art skills for a change. I spent most of the project tinkering with individual values to see what changes, and I quickly understood what works. I had the hardest time playing around with polygons, as there are a lot more aspects that goes into it than circles and rectangles. Creating and placing each individual window on each build was the most time-consuming aspect of this assignment. Overall, I had a lot of fun, and will be utilizing SVG's in my midterm exam (if I can even finish it in time).
